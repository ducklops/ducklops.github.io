AI use.

My videos are not made with A.I. They are made by hand, with love, by me, Harry. I spend hours writing, editing, and designing each one just for you and it would feel like a betrayal to ask a computer to generate it for me.

I understand that A.I. is a controversial and divisive topic in many industries, particularly in the creative sector, which is why I feel obliged to write you this note. To be completely transparent, the website you are currently using was coded with the help of A.I. I am hopeless at coding. If you ever visited my old website, it was hosted on Google Sites, a drag and drop solution. But, it was ducking ugly. I decided to spend some time designing a new website, but I knew I wouldn't be able to code it. Based on my incredibly limited budget and time, I made the decision to upload my designs to A.I. to get help coding this site that you are using.

I do feel ashamed to not have been able to put some time aside and learn to do this myself, and that I'm not in a position where I could sit down with a webdesigner and make this by hand, but what I have here is a good, usable temporary solution. If Ducklops grows enough, an objective of mine is definitely to remake this with a human. But for now, this is the best, most easily navigable and discoverable solution that does the job I need it to.

In case you saw the no A.I. scraping message in the footer and thought this to be slightly hypocritical considering how the website was made, that notice is there due to Readables, where a lot of the scripts and most importantly opinions I have written are kept.

By no means does this mean I will be using A.I. to make my videos. They will remain forever handcrafted.
