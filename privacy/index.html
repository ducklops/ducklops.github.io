No cookies. No analytics. No ads. No tracking. No logins.

Nothing you click, read or search for on ducklops.com is sent to me. The few things that do touch your data (the web host, YouTube pictures and videos, and emails you choose to send) are explained below.

Who's responsible

This website is run by Ducklops, the YouTube channel, from the UK. For anything to do with your data, email hello@ducklops.com. This policy is written with the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018 in mind.

Hosting (GitHub)

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web server, when you visit, GitHub receives your IP address and basic browser details. GitHub says it logs visitor IP addresses for security purposes. I can't see those logs and don't get any visitor statistics from them. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

YouTube pictures & videos

Video thumbnails are loaded straight from YouTube's image servers, so Google can see your IP address when those pictures load.

Videos on Readables use YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com). The video at the top of an article only loads when you press play. A few articles also have a video in the middle of the text, which loads when you scroll near it. Once a YouTube player loads, YouTube may store data in your browser and handle your data under the Google Privacy Policy.

The subscriber count and latest-video box on the home page come from a file on this site that GitHub updates every few hours. Your browser doesn't contact YouTube for them.

Fonts are hosted on this site too, so there are no calls to Google Fonts.

What's saved in your browser

The site doesn't set cookies. It does save a few tiny notes in your own browser's storage so things work the way you'd expect. They never leave your device and I can't see them.

If you email me

If you write to hello@ducklops.com, I'll have your email address, your name if you include it, and whatever you send. I use it only to read and reply to you. I don't add anyone to a mailing list and I don't share or sell your details. I keep emails only as long as I need them to deal with what you've asked. The legal basis is legitimate interests: you got in touch, and replying needs your email.

Names on the site

The Corrections page sometimes thanks the person who spotted a mistake, and the Memberships page can list channel members' YouTube display names. If your name is up and you'd rather it wasn't, email me and I'll take it down.

Memberships, the shop & socials

Channel memberships are run by YouTube. Payments and account details stay with Google. I only see what YouTube shows creators, such as your display name and tier.

The Ducklops Shop at shop.ducklops.com runs on Fourthwall, which handles orders, payments and delivery. It has its own privacy policy, kept up to date by Fourthwall: Ducklops Shop privacy policy.

Social links (YouTube, Twitch, Instagram, TikTok, Bluesky) take you to those sites, and their own privacy policies apply once you're there.

AI and scraping

This site doesn't use your data for AI. My writing on Readables isn't licensed for AI training, and the site's robots.txt file asks AI training crawlers to stay away. You can read more in AI.TXT.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask to see the personal data I hold about you, have it corrected or deleted, object to how it's used, or ask me to limit it. In practice, the only personal data I'm likely to have is an email you sent me. Email hello@ducklops.com and I'll reply within one month.

If you're not happy with how I've handled your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office: ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint. I'd appreciate the chance to sort it out first, though.

This site doesn't ask for or collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If anything here changes (for example, if a members' area with logins is added), this page will be updated before it goes live, and the date below will change.
